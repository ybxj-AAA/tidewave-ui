About

I care about the tenth time you use a screen.

Most design gets judged on first impressions. I've spent my career on the opposite problem: what a screen feels like on the two-hundredth use, at 11pm, when something has gone wrong.

That usually means fewer flourishes, more default states, and a lot of arguing about error copy.
