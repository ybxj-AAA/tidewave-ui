FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How is this different from a BI tool?

BI tools expect you to model data first and ask questions later. Tidewave assumes you already know the metrics that matter and want them live, shareable, and annotated. If you need arbitrary SQL against a warehouse, keep your BI tool — we're not trying to replace it.

Where is my data stored?

Event data lives in the region you pick at signup (US, EU or APAC). You can also connect a warehouse and keep all raw rows in your own account — we only store query results.

Do you support self-hosting?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. It's a single Docker Compose stack plus your database of choice. Roughly forty minutes end to end.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your account drops to the free tier: one dashboard, 30 days of history, one seat. Nothing is deleted for 90 days, so you can upgrade and pick up where you left off.

Can I export everything?

CSV, JSON, or a direct warehouse sync on any paid plan. If you want to leave, we'll send you a full dump — no support ticket required.
